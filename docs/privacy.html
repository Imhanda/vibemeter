Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

VibeMeter ("we", "our", or "the app") is a nightlife vibe-scoring app that lets users check in to nearby venues using short ambient audio recordings. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Microphone Access & Audio Data

When you tap Check In and choose the automatic scan, the app records about 10 seconds of ambient audio through your device's microphone. That audio is:

  • Transmitted over HTTPS to our processing server — the analysis runs on our server, not only on your device
  • Analysed in memory to extract three numeric signals: crowd energy, music energy, and ambient volume level
  • Discarded immediately after analysis — the recording is never written to disk, logged, or shared, on your device or on our servers

Only the three numeric signals derived from the audio are stored, never the audio itself. We do not perform speech recognition and cannot identify speakers or conversations. You can skip the microphone entirely and rate the vibe manually with an emoji scale.

2. Location Access

The app requests access to your device's location (while the app is in use) to:

  • Show you venues near your current position
  • Verify you are within range of a venue before allowing a check-in

Your precise location is used in real time for these purposes. We do not build a history of your locations or share your location with third parties.

3. Account Information

You sign in with Sign in with Apple or your Google account via Firebase Authentication. We receive your name and email address (or Apple's private relay email, if you choose to hide it) solely to create and identify your account. We do not access your contacts, calendar, photos, or any other data from Apple or Google.

3a. Push Notifications

If you turn on notifications for a venue, we store a device push token (via Expo's push service and Apple Push Notification service / Firebase Cloud Messaging) so we can alert you when that venue's score rises above your chosen threshold. Tokens are deleted when you unfollow all venues or delete your account.

4. Check-In Data

When you submit a check-in, we store:

  • The three numeric vibe signals (crowd energy, music energy, ambient level)
  • The venue ID and approximate timestamp
  • Your account identifier (to enforce rate limits and calculate trust scores)

This data is used to compute real-time vibe scores for venues and to award in-app badges. It is not sold or shared with advertisers.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Live vibe scores are computed over a rolling 3-hour window. Individual check-in records are retained to compute venue history and trust scores.

You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Account & Data → Delete Account inside the app. When you do:

  • Your account, name, email, badges, streaks, venue follows and push tokens are permanently removed.
  • Your past check-ins are kept but anonymised — the link to your account is severed — so aggregate venue scores stay accurate.
  • Residual copies in encrypted backups are purged within 30 days.

You may also email us (below) to request deletion.

6. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication and account management. Google's privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Sign in with Apple — authentication. Apple's privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Expo push notifications — delivery of venue alerts you opt into
  • OpenStreetMap & Google Places — venue names, locations and public ratings used to populate the map

We do not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or crash reporting services that collect personal data.

7. Children's Privacy

VibeMeter is intended for users aged 17 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 17.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
