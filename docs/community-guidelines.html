Community Guidelines

Last updated: September 9, 2026

VibeMeter only works if check-ins are honest. These guidelines apply to everyone using the app.

Do

  • Check in only when you are physically at the venue.
  • Rate what you actually experience right now — crowd, music, energy.
  • Report scores that look wrong, spammed, or unsafe, or venues that have closed.

Don't

  • Submit check-ins for a venue you are not at.
  • Automate, script, or bulk-submit check-ins.
  • Coordinate with others to inflate or tank a venue's score.
  • Use the app to harass a venue, its staff, or other users.
  • Post unlawful, hateful, or deliberately misleading content.

How we enforce this

New accounts carry less weight until they build a track record. Check-ins that are far from the venue, statistically implausible, or part of a coordinated pattern are automatically down-weighted or flagged. Accounts that repeatedly break these rules can be suspended or removed.

Reporting

Use the “Report” option on any venue screen (the … menu). Choose a reason — score is wrong, venue has closed, fake check-ins, or safety concern — and add detail if you can. We review reports within 24 hours and take action on the venue's data or the accounts involved as needed.
