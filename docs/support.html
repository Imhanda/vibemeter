VibeMeter Support

Last updated: September 10, 2026

VibeMeter shows real-time, crowd-sourced "vibe scores" for nearby bars, pubs, clubs and restaurants, based on short ambient-sound check-ins and manual ratings from other users.

Need help?
Email vipinhanda2013@gmail.com — we usually reply within 2 business days. Please include your device model, iOS version, and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Frequently asked questions

The venue list is empty or nothing has a score

Pull down to refresh. In a new area it can take a few seconds to load venue data. During the day, tap Last night to see when places were busy, or follow a venue to be notified when it heats up. VibeMeter needs an internet connection — it does not work offline (it will show your last venue list with a stale-data notice).

How does the microphone check-in work? Is my audio recorded?

When you choose the automatic check-in, the app records about 10 seconds of ambient sound, sends it over HTTPS to our server to measure crowd noise and music energy, and discards it immediately. The recording is never stored on your device or ours, and there is no speech recognition. You can skip the microphone entirely and rate the vibe with an emoji scale.

The app says I'm "too far" to check in

Check-ins are only accepted within a short distance of the venue, so scores reflect people who are actually there. Make sure location access is set to "While Using the App" in iOS Settings → VibeMeter.

I can only check in twice per venue per hour

That limit keeps a single person from swinging a venue's score. It resets on a rolling hourly basis.

A venue's score is wrong, or the place has closed

Open the venue, tap the … menu (top right), and choose Report. Pick a reason — score is wrong, venue has closed, fake check-ins, or safety concern — and add detail. We review every report within 24 hours.

How do I delete my account?

Open the Profile tab → Account & Data → Delete Account. Your account, name, email and follows are removed; your past check-ins are kept but anonymised so venue scores stay accurate. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Notifications aren't arriving

Check that notifications are enabled for VibeMeter in iOS Settings, and that you've tapped the bell on at least one venue. Alerts fire when a followed venue's score rises above your threshold.
